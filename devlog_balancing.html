Hearts Extreme System Design July 25, 2026

[DevLog #03] Balancing 10 Extreme Decks & Special Modifier Cards

In Hearts Extreme, players experience a 100% perfect information card puzzle where every player's hand is open to inspection. But having static card decks alone can lead to deterministic repetition. To solve this, we introduced Special Modifier Cards and 10 distinct Deck Modes!

Rank Inversion (↑ Card)

When played, Rank Inversion temporarily flips the strength of every card in the current trick: lower numbers beat higher numbers. This opens up tactical counter-plays where low cards suddenly dominate the field.

Double Score (M*2 Card)

Double Score cards double the penalty or reward points of the current trick, forcing players to recalculate optimal moves mid-round.

Each of the 10 Deck Modes—ranging from 5-suit Space decks to Mono-Heart decks—requires players to adapt their puzzle-solving strategies continuously. We are excited for you to test these decks in our upcoming Steam build!
