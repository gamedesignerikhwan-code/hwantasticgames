Hearts Extreme System Design July 25, 2026

Balancing 10 Extreme Decks & Special Cards

In Hearts Extreme, players experience a 100% perfect information card puzzle where every player's hand is open to inspection. But a single standard deck alone would make puzzles feel repetitive over time. To solve this, we introduced Special Cards and 10 distinct decks!

Dragon & Fairy (↑ / ↓ Cards)

The Dragon (↑) is the strongest card of all, and the Fairy (↓) is the weakest. But when both land in the same trick, the Fairy wins. Holding the Fairy turns an opponent's Dragon from a sure win into a risk.

Bomb (X Card)

When a Bomb is played, no one wins the trick. It lets you erase a trick you can't afford to take, or deny a trick an opponent was counting on.

Each of the 10 decks, ranging from the 5-suit Space Deck to the single-suit Heart Deck, requires players to adapt their puzzle-solving strategies continuously. We are excited for you to test these decks in our upcoming Steam build!
