Hearts Extreme Launch Announcement November 05, 2026

[DevLog #03] Hearts Extreme Officially Released on Steam!

I am thrilled to announce that Hearts Extreme is officially OUT NOW on Steam!

What started earlier this spring as a curious leap into the Vibe Coding era has culminated in my very first solo digital video game launch on Steam. For someone who spent over a decade designing physical tabletop board games and over twenty years in video game production, holding this completed Steam release in my hands feels like reaching a cherished dream milestone.

Hearts Extreme is available now worldwide on Steam for $9.99 USD.

1. The Core Philosophy: Determinism Meets Trick-Taking

Traditional trick-taking card games—whether Hearts, Spades, or Bridge—rely heavily on imperfect information and luck of the deal. In physical board games, that unpredictability creates social tension. But in a dedicated solo digital puzzle game, having a great run ruined by bad RNG can feel frustrating.

With Hearts Extreme, I asked a simple question: What if every player's hand was 100% face up from trick one?

Suddenly, Hearts transforms from a card-passing guessing game into a deep deterministic chess match. You can calculate every lead, manipulate AI discard habits, deliberately force opponents into trick traps, or engineer brilliant "Shoot the Moon" sweeps where you capture all 13 hearts and the Queen of Spades for zero penalties.

2. Packed with Launch Content

We built the full release edition with rich variety and long-term replayability:

  • 10 Rule-Bending Decks: Master everything from the Standard 52-card Classic and the thrilling 6-player Space Deck to Reverse Scoring, Zero Queen, and intense 1-Card Sudden Death duels.
  • Daily Ranked Mode: Every 24 hours, all players around the globe tackle the exact same seeded puzzle. Compare your optimal trick strategies and fight for top placement on the global Steam leaderboards.
  • 16 Languages Fully Localized: English, Korean, German, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Portuguese, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, Hindi, and Arabic.
  • 50+ Steam Achievements: Satisfying milestones celebrating strategic mastery, puzzle milestones, and deck completion.
  • Steam Cloud Integration: Pick up your runs and unlocked decks seamlessly across multiple PCs and Steam Deck devices.

The 6-Player Space Deck introduces unique suits and higher stakes.

3. A Heartfelt Thank You to the Community

I want to express my deepest gratitude to everyone who played the early web prototypes, shared thoughtful balancing feedback on Discord, and supported this solo indie endeavor. Hearing from trick-taking fans who fell in love with the open-hand puzzle format kept me fueled through late-night coding and UI polishing sessions.

Whether you are an experienced Hearts veteran or someone who loves deductive logic puzzles like Sudoku and Minesweeper, I hope Hearts Extreme gives you many hours of satisfying mental exercise.
