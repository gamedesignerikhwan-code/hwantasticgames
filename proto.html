Tamers — Game Overview

🎯 Game Overview

Tamers is an exact-bidding trick-taking card game designed for 2 to 4 players. After examining their starting hand, each player bids on the exact number of tricks they intend to take this round.

🃏 Core Mechanics

  • Exact Bidding: Bonus points are awarded only when you hit your exact prediction. Taking more or fewer tricks than your bid incurs penalty points.
  • Lead Suit & Trump: Strict must-follow suit rules paired with a dynamic trump system that allows tactical comebacks.
  • Special Ability Cards: Unique ability cards that let players manipulate trick flow beyond raw numerical values.

Doore — Game Overview

🌾 Game Overview

Doore is a 2 to 4 player tile-placement strategy board game rooted in the traditional Korean cooperative farming institution ('두레'). Players balance cooperation and competition to pool community labor and connect tiles for village prosperity.

🧩 Core Mechanics

  • Tile Placement & Path Synergy: Draft and lay rice paddies, farm fields, village hubs, and waterways to maximize connectivity and scoring bonuses.
  • Communal Labor & Resource Allocation: Wisely manage worker units and coins to complete large communal projects and share harvest rewards.
  • Strategic Interaction: Balance shared community milestones with individual scoring opportunities across evolving village layouts.

Waffle Pop! — Game Overview

🧇 Game Overview

Waffle Pop! is a delightful tactical dessert board game for 2 to 4 players. On a 4×4 waffle plate stacked with 3 layers of delicious topping tiles (48 tiles total), players compete to fulfill spatial pattern mission cards and harvest high-scoring toppings.

🧩 Core Mechanics

  • 3D Grid & Spatial Pattern Matching: Draft coordinate cards to match 3, 4, or 5-cell pattern missions on the waffle plate (full support for rotations and mirrored reflections).
  • Multi-Tiered Topping Harvest: Fulfilling a mission lets you claim the top topping from each matched coordinate, gradually revealing deeper layers and creating dynamic board shifts.
  • Asymmetric Topping Scoring: Strategic variety across 7 topping types—from first-come first-served VIP ribbons and Golden Cherry sets to quadratic Strawberry combos and shared dividends.
  • Equal-Turn Endgame: Triggered when 5 or more spots on the 4×4 waffle plate are fully cleared of all 3 layers.

Orchards — Game Overview

🍏 Game Overview

Orchards (오차드) is an engaging 2 to 5 player tile-drafting and spatial orchard-building board game. Over 13 dynamic rounds, players secretly bid hand tiles to determine market draft priority, expand their personal orchard grids with split-terrain tiles, and cultivate bountiful fruit harvests.

🧩 Core Mechanics

  • Simultaneous Secret Bidding & Market Draft: Each round, players secretly select a tile from hand. Lower numbered bids draft earlier from the revealed market tiles, creating sharp tactical priority decisions.
  • Split-Terrain Placement & Rotation: Rotate dual-colored diagonal terrain tiles in 90° increments to connect matching colors and expand your grove.
  • Crop Harvesting & Worker Multipliers: Bidding crop tiles grants fruit tokens placed onto matching colored orchards. Final scoring multiplies workers by fruit tokens across your clusters.
  • Wild Scarecrows & Area Majority: Leverage wild Scarecrow (🎃) tokens on any terrain color and race to earn +10 bonus points for the largest worker group in each color.

Rotato — Game Overview

🥔 Game Overview

Rotato (로타토) is an addictive spatial puzzle and gravity tile-placement board game for 2 to 4 players. Players draft tiles into personal 3-column boards like Tetris, ignite chain-reaction edge rotations, and score completed tiles as they complete full 3-step rotations.

🧩 Core Mechanics

  • Gravity Column Drop: On each turn, pick 1 hand tile and drop it into one of your 3 columns. The tile falls to the lowest available space with its IN mark facing downward.
  • Edge-Matching Rotation Chains: If touching edges match colors/symbols after placement, rotate matching tiles clockwise. Chain reactions continue until no adjacent matches remain.
  • Type-Match Bonus Rotation: After resolving matching chains, you may rotate 1 tile sharing the same potato/topping icon as the placed tile, sparking new potential chains.
  • 3-Turn Completion & Gravity Collapse: When a tile completes 3 clockwise rotations and its OUT mark points lower-right, it becomes complete! Pressing complete moves it to your score pile, and all tiles above drop downward by gravity.
  • Clockwise Drafting & Set Scoring: Pass remaining hand tiles clockwise after each turn. At game end, score points equal to each icon type squared, plus 10 bonus points for each complete set of 4 icons.
