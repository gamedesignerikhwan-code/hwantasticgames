ABOUT BOOMSIDE OVERVIEW

Boomside is a roguelike twist on Minesweeper where every square scores. Dig an 8×8 lot to uncover Houses (1, 2, 3…), Signal Towers (1, 4, 9…), Parks (safe-dig streak), and Power Plants while reading Minesweeper-style hints to avoid buried gas mains.

Between lots, navigate the city map across shops, banks, supply stations, and random incidents. Hire from 161 Helpers, combine 24 round items, 16 map items, 16 permits, and 29 cell items, and secure all eight key sites across 64 district-and-tier combinations.

ABOUT THE DEVELOPER HWANTASTIC GAMES

Hwantastic Games is a solo indie video game studio based in South Korea, founded by an industry veteran with over 20 years of video game experience and nearly a decade as a board game designer.
Inspired by Korean music artist Lee Seung Hwan's 9th studio album Hwantastic, the studio builds focused, puzzle-driven strategy titles on Steam.
