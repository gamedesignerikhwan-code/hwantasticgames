Hwantastic Games Studio DevLog August 3, 2026

[DevLog #01] Building the Hwantastic Games Website in the Vibe Coding Era

The official website of Hwantastic Games has been open to the public since August 2026. In the current era of Vibe Coding, creating a website with a single prompt or click has become a reality. However, when it comes to shaping a website into the exact form you desire, you ultimately realize that you must carefully specify your own requirements and specifications for every single menu and layout element.

While researching websites of various game studios to design our company hub, the layout structure that inspired me the most was that of Mega Crit. Initially, when our first prototype was generated in a single click, all information was stacked vertically on one long scrolling page, with the top navigation acting merely as anchor bookmarks.

Instead of infinite smooth scrolling, we transitioned to tab-based navigation while trimming away unnecessary menus. This naturally brought our structure closer to Mega Crit's clean layout. While they host multiple games, we currently focus on one core title, making our layout even leaner and more streamlined.

After stripping away the clutter, the resulting clean and focused design feels very satisfying. We plan to continuously polish and improve it moving forward.

From now on, this space will serve as the base hub where we consistently share news, development logs, patch notes, and design insights about our games. We will work hard to bring you great experiences, so please stay tuned and follow our journey!
