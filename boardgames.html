Pyramido

In Print 🏆 2024 Mensa Ludo Winner
Publisher: Synapses Games (2023)

A multi-level tile placement strategy game where players construct a 4-level pyramid by matching jewel icons and colored markers.
Awarded the prestigious 2024 Mensa Ludo Award for its inventive multi-tier scoring puzzle.

2-4 Players 45 Min Age 8+ Tile Placement / Pyramid Construction

Alice's Garden

In Print
Publisher: Lifestyle Boardgames (2020)

A spatial polyomino drafting puzzle game themed around Alice in Wonderland.
Players cultivate mushrooms, Cheshire cats, and rose trees according to the Queen of Hearts' whimsical gardening rules.
Published globally in over 10 languages.

1-4 Players 30-45 Min Age 8+ Polyomino Spatial Puzzle

Surfosaurus MAX

In Print 🏆 2025 Mensa Recommended
Publisher: Loosey Goosey Games (2023)

A fast-paced semi-cooperative card game where surfing dinosaurs build poker-like hand combinations using shared community cards in the center of the table.
Players collaborate to construct the ultimate wave combo, but only those who contributed to the winning set score points.

2-6 Players 20 Min Age 8+ Shared Card Combinations / Bluffing

SizeUp

In Print
Publisher: Helvetiq (2018)

An inventive party game featuring an actual retractable tape measure.

Flea Market

In Print
Publisher: Musoka Studio (2025)

A tactical push-your-luck card game set in a vibrant open-air flea market.
Players add items into the central market while keeping an eye on the round's element limit.
Exceeding the limit forces a player to gift items to rivals and hoard the remainder, balancing precious finds against cursed penalties.

2-5 Players 15-30 Min Age 8+ Push Your Luck / Hand Management / Set Collection
