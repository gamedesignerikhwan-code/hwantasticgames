Pyramido

In Print 🏆 2024 Mensa Ludo Winner
Publisher: Synapses Games (2023) · Korean Edition: Board M

A multi-level tile placement strategy game where players construct a 4-level pyramid by matching jewel icons and colored markers.
Awarded the prestigious 2024 Mensa Ludo Award for its inventive multi-tier scoring puzzle.

2-4 Players 45 Min Age 8+ Tile Placement / Pyramid Construction

Alice's Garden

In Print
Publisher: Korea Boardgames / IELLO / Global Partners (2020)

A spatial polyomino drafting puzzle game themed around Alice in Wonderland.
Players cultivate mushrooms, Cheshire cats, and rose trees according to the Queen of Hearts' whimsical gardening rules.
Published globally in over 10 languages.

1-4 Players 30 Min Age 8+ Polyomino Spatial Puzzle

Surfosaurus MAX

In Print

A fast-paced card game where surfing dinosaurs build poker-like hand combinations using shared community cards in the center of the table.
Packed with bluffing, table talk, and clever tactical timing.
