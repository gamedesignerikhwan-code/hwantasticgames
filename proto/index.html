Tamers — Game Overview

🎯 Game Overview

Tamers is an exact-bidding trick-taking card game designed for 2 to 4 players. After examining their starting hand, each player bids on the exact number of tricks they intend to take this round.

🃏 Core Mechanics

  • Exact Bidding: Bonus points are awarded only when you hit your exact prediction. Taking more or fewer tricks than your bid incurs penalty points.
  • Lead Suit & Trump: Strict must-follow suit rules paired with a dynamic trump system that allows tactical comebacks.
  • Special Ability Cards: Unique ability cards that let players manipulate trick flow beyond raw numerical values.

Doore — Game Overview

🌾 Game Overview

Doore is a tile-placement strategy board game rooted in the traditional Korean cooperative farming institution ('두레'). Players balance cooperation and competition to pool community labor and connect tiles for village prosperity.

🧩 Core Mechanics

  • Tile Placement & Path Synergy: Draft and lay rice paddies, farm fields, village hubs, and waterways to maximize connectivity and scoring bonuses.
  • Communal Labor & Resource Allocation: Wisely manage worker units and coins to complete large communal projects and share harvest rewards.
  • Solo AI Mode & Real-time Multiplayer: Test solo anytime against AI worker bots, or generate room codes to play live with other testers over WebRTC.
