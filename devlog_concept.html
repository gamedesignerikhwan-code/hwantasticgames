Hearts Extreme Game Concept July 05, 2026

Why Hearts? Turning Classic Trick-Taking into a Puzzle

Here is the origin story behind Hwantastic Games' debut Steam title, Hearts Extreme.

Traditional trick-taking card games (like Hearts or Spades) rely heavily on card deal luck and hidden hands. We asked ourselves: "What if everyone's hand was 100% fully revealed from turn one? Wouldn't it transform trick-taking into a deep, chess-like puzzle?"

The result is a game where luck no longer decides the outcome on its own: reading every hand and planning ahead does most of the work. Stay tuned for more devlogs as we refine the daily puzzle challenges!
