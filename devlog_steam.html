Hearts Extreme Steam Release July 18, 2026

Steam Integration & 16 Language Localization

We are thrilled to update our community on the Steam integration progress for Hearts Extreme!

Full 16 Language Localization

Card game players around the world deserve a native experience. Hearts Extreme is localized into 16 languages: English, Korean, Deutsch, 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Français, Español, Italiano, Русский, Português, Türkçe, Nederlands, Polski, हिन्दी, and العربية.

Steam Features Integration

We have successfully integrated Steam Cloud Save, Steam Achievements, and Daily Leaderboard Sync. Players can compete daily on global leaderboards and track their personal puzzle solving records.
