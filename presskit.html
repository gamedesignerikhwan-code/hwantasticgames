About the Developer

Hwantastic Games is a solo indie video game studio based in South Korea, founded by an industry veteran with over 20 years of video game experience and nearly a decade as a board game designer.
Inspired by Korean music artist Lee Seung Hwan's 9th studio album Hwantastic, the studio was established in the Vibe Coding era to craft unique puzzle-driven card games.
Hwantastic Games strives to deliver innovative experiences at the intersection of developer passion and player enjoyment.
